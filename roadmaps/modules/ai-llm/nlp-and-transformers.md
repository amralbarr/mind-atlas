# Module: NLP & Transformer Architecture

**Category:** AI / [LLM](../../../engineering/concepts/ai/generation/llm.md) · **Typical duration:** 2 weeks (15–20 hrs/week) · **Prerequisite modules:** [Deep Learning Foundations](../data-and-ml/deep-learning-foundations.md)

## Goal

Explain how a modern LLM works, from a raw string to an output token, well enough to answer "how does attention work?" without notes.

## Core topics

- [ ] Tokenization: BPE, WordPiece, SentencePiece; why some languages/scripts cost more tokens than others
- [ ] Word and contextual [embeddings](../../../engineering/concepts/ai/ingestion/embeddings.md)
- [ ] Self-attention, multi-head attention, positional encodings, layer norm, residual connections
- [ ] Encoder-only vs decoder-only vs encoder-decoder architectures and what each is good for
- [ ] Classic [NLP](../../../engineering/concepts/ai/nlp.md) tasks: classification, NER, summarization, question answering

## Resources

- The original transformer architecture paper, read alongside a well-known visual companion explainer
- A hands-on "build a tiny GPT from scratch" exercise/video
- A structured free LLM/transformers course covering tokenizers, models, and datasets

## Milestone deliverable

A from-scratch (or near-scratch) mini-transformer implementation trained on a toy task, plus a short written explanation of self-attention in your own words.

## Related concepts

Tokenization, LLM, NLP
