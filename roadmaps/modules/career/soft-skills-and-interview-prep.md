# Module: Soft Skills & Interview Prep

**Category:** Career · **Typical duration:** ~1 hour/week throughout, plus a dedicated 2–4 week final phase · **Prerequisite modules:** none — the ongoing half runs alongside every other module; the final phase comes last

## Goal

Communicate technical work to both engineers and non-technical stakeholders, and walk into an interview ready for [system design](../../../engineering/concepts/backend/architecture/system-design.md), coding, and behavioral questions.

## Core topics (ongoing, throughout the roadmap)

- [ ] Write one short design doc (context, options, tradeoffs, decision) for a real design choice you made
- [ ] Practice explaining one technical concept to a non-technical audience in under 2 minutes
- [ ] If the target seniority includes leading/mentoring: run one internal talk or write one short post per phase

## Core topics (final interview-prep phase)

- [ ] System design: practice 4–6 realistic designs end to end for the target role, covering requirements, architecture, scaling, security, and cost
- [ ] Coding practice: a set of medium-difficulty problems, plus building something small and live under a time limit
- [ ] Deep-dive questions: write out your own answers to the hardest "how does X work / when would you choose X over Y" questions the role could ask, and say them out loud
- [ ] Behavioral stories in STAR format: leading a team, a production incident, a disagreement with a stakeholder, mentoring someone, delivering under a deadline, a failure and what changed afterward
- [ ] Mock interviews with a peer or a practice platform; record at least one and review it
- [ ] Prepare your own questions for the interviewer (team size, ownership scope, tooling, what "success" looks like in the first 90 days)

## Resources

- A free, widely-used system design interview guide for the design-question format
- A mock-interview practice platform or a willing peer

## Milestone deliverable

Six fully written behavioral stories (STAR format) and six practiced system-design answers for the target role, plus at least one recorded mock interview reviewed afterward.
