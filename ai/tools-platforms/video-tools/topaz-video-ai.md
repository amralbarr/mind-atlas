# Topaz Video AI

**Type:** Paid — subscription (Personal ~$299/yr, Pro ~$699/yr; monthly options also available)

## Category
Video upscaling, frame interpolation, denoising, and restoration (desktop app + API)

## AI Capabilities
- Upscaling to 4K/8K (Personal tier) or up to 24K (Pro tier)
- Frame interpolation up to 120fps for smooth slow-motion or frame-rate conversion
- Denoising, deinterlacing, and stabilization (full-frame and auto-crop modes)
- A 2026 lineup of 19+ specialized models for different jobs — including models aimed specifically at cleaning up artifacts from AI-generated video, not just traditional footage
- A separate pay-per-use "Topaz Enhance API" for programmatic/batch use outside the desktop app
