# Real-CUGAN

**Type:** Open source (MIT) — by Bilibili AI Lab

## Category
Video/image super-resolution (upscaling), anime-focused

## AI Capabilities
- Super-resolution tuned specifically for anime/illustration-style video and images (2x/3x/4x)
- Faster inference than [Real-ESRGAN](real-esrgan.md) on anime-style content
- Self-hosted; kept usable via community ports (ncnn-vulkan, VapourSynth plugin) that run on non-NVIDIA GPUs too

## Related AI Solutions
- [Video Upscaling, Interpolation & Restoration](../../solutions/video/video-enhancement/video-upscaling-interpolation-and-restoration.md)

## Notes
- Pick this over Real-ESRGAN specifically for anime/cartoon content; Real-ESRGAN is the better general-purpose default for live-action footage.
