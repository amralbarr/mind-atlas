# FlowFrames

**Type:** Open source / donationware (source available free; supporter builds released early)

## Category
Video frame interpolation (desktop GUI)

## AI Capabilities
- Windows GUI that wraps [RIFE](rife.md) (and DAIN/FLAVR) so frame interpolation doesn't require the command line
- Batch-processes video files to raise frame rate or produce smooth slow-motion

## Related AI Solutions
- [Video Upscaling, Interpolation & Restoration](../../solutions/video/video-enhancement/video-upscaling-interpolation-and-restoration.md)

## Notes
- A fully-free fork, OpenFlowFrames, has also emerged with no paid-tier gating.
- The practical on-ramp for someone who wants RIFE's quality without setting up Python/CUDA themselves.
