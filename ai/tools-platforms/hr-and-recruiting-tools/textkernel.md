# Textkernel (formerly Sovren)

**Type:** Paid (B2B API, usage-based)

## Category
Resume/CV parsing engine

## AI Capabilities
- Extracts structured data from resumes (name, skills, experience, education) across many languages
- Powers the "AI screening" feature inside many ATS products that don't build parsing in-house
