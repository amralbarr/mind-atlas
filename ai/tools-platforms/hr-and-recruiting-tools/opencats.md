# OpenCATS

**Type:** Open source (free)

## Category
Applicant Tracking System (ATS) — self-hosted

## AI Capabilities
- None built in by default — this is a classic ATS (PHP/LAMP stack), not an AI-native product
- Listed here as the realistic self-hosted baseline a team pairs with a separate AI resume-screening tool (e.g. [Resume Matcher](resume-matcher.md) or a parsing API like [Textkernel](textkernel.md))

## Related AI Solutions

## Notes
- Full data ownership, no per-seat fees, actively maintained as of 2026. The realistic open-source alternative to [Greenhouse](greenhouse.md)/[Ashby](ashby.md) for teams prioritizing self-hosting over built-in AI features.
