# Resume Matcher

**Type:** Open source (Apache 2.0)

## Category
Resume-to-job-description scoring tool

## AI Capabilities
- Scores and aligns a resume against a specific job description, highlighting gaps
- Runs locally, no data leaves the machine

## Related AI Solutions
- [Resume Screening & Candidate Matching](../../solutions/documents/document-understanding/resume-screening-and-candidate-matching.md)

## Notes
- Originally built as a candidate-side tool (helping a job seeker tailor their resume) as much as a recruiter-side screening tool.
- As of 2026, no production-grade open-source resume *parser* matches paid APIs like [Textkernel](textkernel.md) at scale — open source is realistic for scoring/matching, not high-volume parsing infrastructure.
