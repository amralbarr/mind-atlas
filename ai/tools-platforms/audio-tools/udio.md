# Udio

**Type:** Paid (subscription)

## Category
AI full-song generation (text-to-music with vocals)

## AI Capabilities
- Generates complete songs with AI vocals and instrumentation from a text prompt, similar positioning to Suno

## Notes
- Was sued by major labels over training-data use; **settled with Universal Music Group (October 29, 2025)** to jointly build a new licensed AI music platform, and **settled with Warner Music Group (November 19, 2025)**. Both settlements pair compensation with new licensing deals, and the resulting licensed platform is expected to restrict users from downloading/distributing outputs off-platform.
- Sony's litigation against Udio was still active as of mid-2026 (status conference scheduled for May 29, 2026) — verify current status before treating all Udio output as commercially clear.
