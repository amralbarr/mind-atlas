# Stable Audio

**Type:** Open source (Small SFX/Small/Medium — Stability AI Community License) + Paid (Large model, API, self-hosting)

## Category
Generative audio — music and sound-effects model family by Stability AI

## AI Capabilities
- Stable Audio 3.0 (May 2026) is a four-model family: Small SFX, Small, Medium (open-weight), and Large (paid API/self-host only)
- Trained exclusively on licensed and Creative Commons audio — a deliberate differentiator on copyright-clean training data
- Covers both music composition and sound-effects/ambience generation

## Notes
- The Stability AI Community License allows free commercial use of the open-weight models up to $1M in annual revenue; beyond that, an Enterprise license is required (which also adds legal indemnification for copyright claims on model output).
- Commercial use under the Community License still requires registering with Stability AI — it isn't a no-strings-attached open license.
- The Large model is reserved for the paid API/self-hosting tier and isn't released as open weights.
