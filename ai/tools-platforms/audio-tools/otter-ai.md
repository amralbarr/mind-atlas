# Otter.ai

**Type:** Paid (subscription; free tier available)

## Category
Meeting-transcription and note-taking app (consumer/business, not a developer API)

## AI Capabilities
- Live meeting transcription with speaker labels, auto-generated summaries, and action-item extraction
- Calendar/video-conferencing integrations (Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams) for auto-join transcription

## Related AI Solutions
- [AI Transcription (Speech-to-Text)](../../solutions/audio/speech-to-text/ai-transcription.md)

## Notes
- 2026 pricing: free Basic plan (300 min/month, 30-min cap per conversation, 3 lifetime imports); Pro $16.99/mo (or $8.33/mo billed annually, 1,200 min/month); Business $30/mo (or $19.99/mo annually, unlimited minutes, 3-seat minimum); custom-priced Enterprise.
- An end-user product built on STT, not infrastructure to build on — compare to [Deepgram](deepgram.md)/[AssemblyAI](assemblyai.md) for a developer-facing API.
