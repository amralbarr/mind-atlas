# Google Lyria 2

**Type:** Paid (Vertex AI usage-based)

## Category
Enterprise instrumental music generation API

## AI Capabilities
- Text-to-music generation with controls for key, BPM, and style, plus negative prompts to exclude unwanted elements (e.g. "vocals," "distorted guitar")
- Extends existing audio tracks while preserving tone/style
- Outputs professional-grade 48kHz stereo audio (clips up to ~32.8 seconds, up to 4 per prompt)
- Ships with the Music AI Sandbox for interactive composition

## Notes
- Generally available on Vertex AI alongside Veo (video) and Imagen (image) as Google's enterprise generative-media lineup.
- Positioned for enterprise/production use rather than consumer song creation — no AI-sung full-song vocals in the way Suno/Udio offer.
