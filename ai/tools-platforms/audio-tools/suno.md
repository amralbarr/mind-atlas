# Suno

**Type:** Paid (subscription; free tier)

## Category
AI full-song generation (text-to-music with vocals)

## AI Capabilities
- Generates complete songs — lyrics, AI-sung vocals, and instrumentation — from a text prompt
- Suno Studio for extended editing/production workflows (Premier tier)

## Notes
- 2026 pricing: Pro $8/month, Premier $24/month (annual billing; monthly billing runs higher, around $10/$30). Download allowances: free accounts get 7 lifetime trial downloads, Pro 20/month, Premier 60/month (unlimited in Suno Studio).
- Signed major-label licensing deals through 2026: Warner Music Group (settled + partnered, November 2025), BMG (global partnership, August 2026), Believe/TuneCore (September 2026). Universal and Sony litigation status should be re-verified, as it was still evolving through 2026.
- Commercial-use safety of generated output depends on which label catalogs are covered by current licensing deals — verify before recommending for commercial release.
