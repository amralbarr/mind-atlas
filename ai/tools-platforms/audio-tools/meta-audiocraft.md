# Meta AudioCraft (MusicGen / AudioGen)

**Type:** Open source (MIT code; CC-BY-NC-4.0 weights)

## Category
Self-hosted generative audio framework — music (MusicGen) and sound effects/ambience (AudioGen)

## AI Capabilities
- MusicGen: text- and melody-conditioned music generation
- AudioGen: text-to-sound-effects generation (ambient noise, foley-style sounds)
- EnCodec: the neural audio codec/tokenizer underlying both models
- MusicGen was trained on ~20,000 hours of music either owned by Meta or specifically licensed for training
