# Google Cloud Text-to-Speech

**Type:** Paid (usage-based, per-character/token; free monthly allowance)

## Category
Cloud text-to-speech API

## AI Capabilities
- Tiered voice quality: Standard, WaveNet, Neural2, Chirp 3 HD, Studio, Instant Custom Voice
- Gemini-TTS models (Gemini 2.5 Pro TTS, Gemini 3.1/3.8 Flash TTS) with token-based pricing and more controllable, conversational delivery
- Broad language/accent coverage

## Notes
- 2026 pricing per 1M characters: Standard/WaveNet $4, Neural2 $16, Chirp 3 HD $30, Instant Custom Voice $60, Studio $160. First 1M characters free per month (varies by tier).
- Gemini-TTS models are priced per token instead: e.g. Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS (preview) at $0.50/1M text tokens through end of 2026, rising to $1.00/1M in 2027; Gemini 2.5 Pro TTS at $1.00/1M text tokens + $20/1M audio tokens.
