# Respeecher

**Type:** Paid (enterprise/project-based, custom pricing)

## Category
Voice cloning for film, TV, and media production

## AI Capabilities
- High-fidelity voice conversion/cloning for dubbing, ADR, and localization
- Consent-first workflow: requires documented consent from the voice owner before cloning
- Applies audio watermarking and is developing synthetic-speech detection to deter misuse

## Notes
- Positions itself explicitly around ethical/consent-based use — a differentiator from more open self-serve cloning tools, and the standard pick when a studio needs an actor's own cloned voice for dubbing/ADR rather than a generic replacement voice.
- Pricing is project/enterprise-based rather than a published self-serve plan.
