# PlayAI (formerly Play.ht)

**Type:** Formerly paid SaaS — acquired, no longer independently available

## Category
Text-to-speech / conversational voice platform (historical)

## AI Capabilities
- Was a TTS and real-time conversational-voice-model platform prior to acquisition

## Notes
- Play.ht rebranded to PlayAI as it expanded into real-time voice models and conversational agents, then was **acquired by Meta in July 2025** and absorbed into Meta's AI organization. It is no longer offered as an independent product — do not recommend it as an active vendor option as of November 2026.
- Kept here as a historical/market-note entry; evaluate [ElevenLabs](elevenlabs.md) or [OpenAI TTS](openai-tts.md) as current alternatives instead.
