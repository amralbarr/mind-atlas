# Fathom

**Type:** Paid — genuinely unlimited Free tier; Premium $16/user/mo, Team $15/user/mo, Business $25/user/mo (annual billing)

## Category
AI meeting notetaker

## AI Capabilities
- Unlimited recording, transcription, and storage on the Free plan across Zoom/Google Meet/Microsoft Teams
- AI summaries and highlight reels — Free gives advanced AI summaries on the first 5 calls/month, then falls back to a generic template
- Paid tiers unlock unlimited advanced AI summaries plus CRM sync and team features

## Notes
- One of the few meeting-notetaker tools with a genuinely uncapped free recording/transcription tier; the real limit on Free is advanced AI summary volume, not recording time.
