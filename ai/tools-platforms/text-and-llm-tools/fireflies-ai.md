# Fireflies.ai

**Type:** Paid — Free tier; Pro $10/user/mo annual ($18 monthly); Business $19/user/mo annual ($29 monthly); Enterprise $39/user/mo annual

## Category
AI meeting recording, transcription, and summarization

## AI Capabilities
- Unlimited transcription and AI summaries on every tier, including Free
- Credit-based AI features (summaries, "AskFred" Q&A over transcripts, action-item extraction) — a single meeting can consume several credits if multiple AI features are run on it
- CRM integrations for pushing meeting notes into sales workflows
