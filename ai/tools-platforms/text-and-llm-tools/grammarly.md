# Grammarly

**Type:** Paid — Free tier; Pro $12/mo billed annually ($30/mo monthly); custom Enterprise

## Category
AI writing assistant (grammar, clarity, tone, rewriting)

## AI Capabilities
- Real-time grammar, spelling, tone, and clarity suggestions
- AI rewriting and prompt-based editing (capped at 100 AI prompts/month on Free, 2,000/month on Pro)
- Plagiarism detection and style/tone adjustment on Pro
