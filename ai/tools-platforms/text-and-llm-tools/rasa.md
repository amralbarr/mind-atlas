# Rasa

**Type:** Open source (Apache 2.0)

## Category
Conversational-AI framework (classic NLU pipeline + newer LLM-assisted flows)

## AI Capabilities
- Full control over intents, entities, and dialogue management
- Self-hosted with complete source-code access, no vendor lock-in
- Steeper build curve than no-code builders like [Botpress](botpress.md) or [Chatwoot](chatwoot.md)

## Related AI Solutions
- [AI Customer Support Agents](../../solutions/text/text-to-text/ai-customer-support-agents.md)

## Notes
- The strongest choice for enterprises building a fully custom assistant where off-the-shelf flows aren't enough.
