# Stable Diffusion

**Type:** Open source (Stability AI Community License — free for research and for individuals/organizations under $1M annual revenue; paid Enterprise License above that)

## Category
Text-to-image diffusion model (self-hosted)

## AI Capabilities
- Text-to-image generation, plus image-to-image and inpainting/outpainting when paired with a UI/extension
- Current flagship family is SD 3.5 (Large, Large Turbo, Medium) — Medium runs on consumer GPUs with ~10GB VRAM
- Weights are gated on Hugging Face/Stability's model hub — downloading requires accepting the Community License

## Related AI Solutions
- [Text-to-Image Generation](../../solutions/image/image-generation/text-to-image-generation.md)

## Notes
- Rarely run raw — almost always driven through a workflow UI like [ComfyUI](comfyui.md) or a plugin like [Krita AI Diffusion](krita-ai-diffusion.md).
- No newer "SD 4" exists as of late 2026 — SD 3.5 remains Stability AI's current open image model.
