# GFPGAN

**Type:** Open source (Apache 2.0) — by Tencent ARC Lab

## Category
Face restoration model

## AI Capabilities
- Restores and sharpens old, blurry, or low-resolution faces in photos
- Commonly chained after [Real-ESRGAN](real-esrgan.md) so a full-image upscale also gets proper face restoration

## Notes
