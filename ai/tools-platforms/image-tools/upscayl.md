# Upscayl

**Type:** Open source (AGPL-3.0)

## Category
Desktop app for AI image upscaling

## AI Capabilities
- Free GUI wrapper around [Real-ESRGAN](real-esrgan.md) and other open upscaling models — no command line or coding required
- Batch upscaling of many images at once, running fully offline/locally on Linux, macOS, and Windows

## Related AI Solutions
- [Image Upscaling & Restoration](../../solutions/image/image-enhancement/image-upscaling-and-restoration.md)

## Notes
- The most popular open-source AI image upscaler on GitHub (tens of thousands of stars) — the free, self-hosted alternative to [Topaz Photo AI/Gigapixel](topaz-photo-ai.md) or [Magnific](magnific.md).
