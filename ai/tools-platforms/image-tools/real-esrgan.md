# Real-ESRGAN

**Type:** Open source (Apache 2.0) — by Tencent ARC Lab

## Category
Image/video super-resolution (upscaling) model

## AI Capabilities
- Practical, general-purpose super-resolution tuned for real-world degraded images (compression artifacts, blur, noise), not just clean synthetic downscaling
- The de facto upscaling backend other actively developed tools build on, including [ComfyUI](comfyui.md) and [Upscayl](upscayl.md)

## Related AI Solutions
- [Image Upscaling & Restoration](../../solutions/image/image-enhancement/image-upscaling-and-restoration.md)

## Notes
- Pair with [GFPGAN](gfpgan.md) for face restoration — Real-ESRGAN alone is not specialized for faces.
- A model/library, not an end-user app — use [Upscayl](upscayl.md) for a free GUI, or [ComfyUI](comfyui.md) to wire it into a larger pipeline.
