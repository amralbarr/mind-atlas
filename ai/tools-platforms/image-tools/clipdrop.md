# Clipdrop

**Type:** Paid (freemium web app + API; owned by Jasper since February 2024)

## Category
Web/API image-editing toolkit

## AI Capabilities
- Cleanup — object/text removal from a photo
- Uncrop — AI outpainting to extend an image beyond its original frame
- Relight, background removal, and image upscaling

## Related AI Solutions
- [AI-Powered Image Editing](../../solutions/image/image-editing/ai-image-editing-and-object-removal.md)

## Notes
- Originally built by InitML, acquired by Stability AI in 2023, then sold to Jasper in February 2024 — still commonly (and now inaccurately) referred to as "Stability AI's Clipdrop." Stability AI has continued supplying some generative models to the platform under an ongoing partnership.
