# Adobe Firefly

**Type:** Paid ($9.99–$199.99/month standalone across Standard/Pro/Pro Plus/Premium tiers; also bundled into Creative Cloud/Photoshop)

## Category
Text-to-image generation and in-app generative editing engine

## AI Capabilities
- Text-to-image generation via the Firefly web app, trained on licensed/Adobe Stock content and marketed as commercially safe for enterprise use
- Powers Photoshop's Generative Fill and Generative Expand (inpainting/outpainting directly inside Photoshop) and Illustrator's Generative Recolor
- Credit-based usage for premium features; standard Generative Fill with Adobe's own models runs unlimited on any paid Firefly/Creative Cloud Pro plan
- A free tier offers a limited number of daily generations on a curated set of models
