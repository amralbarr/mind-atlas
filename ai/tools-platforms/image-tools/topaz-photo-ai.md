# Topaz Photo AI / Gigapixel AI

**Type:** Paid (subscription-only since September 2025 — Gigapixel from ~$149/year standalone; the Topaz Studio bundle, which adds Photo AI, Video AI, Bloom, and Astra, from ~$399/year)

## Category
Desktop AI photo enhancement suite (by Topaz Labs)

## AI Capabilities
- Gigapixel AI — upscaling to very high resolutions (up to 16x+ depending on mode)
- Photo AI — automatic one-pass denoising, sharpening, and face recovery/restoration
- Runs locally on the user's machine rather than as a cloud API

## Notes
- Topaz Labs ended perpetual (one-time-purchase) licenses in 2025; the full lineup is subscription-only as of 2026.
- Compare to free, open-source [Upscayl](upscayl.md)/[Real-ESRGAN](real-esrgan.md) for a no-cost alternative, or [Magnific](magnific.md) for a cloud, "creative" (detail-hallucinating) upscaler.
