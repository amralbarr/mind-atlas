# AI-Driven Interviews (Chat, Voice & Video)

## Description
AI that conducts a candidate interview directly — asking questions, adapting follow-ups, and scoring responses — across chat, phone/voice, or two-way video, instead of a human recruiter running the live interview. Distinct from "interview intelligence" tools (e.g. BrightHire, Metaview) that record/transcribe a *human-led* interview and coach the recruiter; the tools below replace the live interviewer for screening-stage interviews.

## Input
- Job requirements / interview question bank
- Candidate responses (text, speech, or video)

## Output
- A transcript/recording
- A structured score per competency, usually with written reasoning (for explainability/compliance)
- A recommendation to advance or reject

## Applications
- High-volume first-round screening (retail, hourly, BPO/call-center hiring)
- 24/7 candidate scheduling flexibility (no live recruiter slot needed)
- Standardized scoring across a large candidate pool

## Compliance note
AI-conducted interviews and automated scoring are directly regulated in some jurisdictions — e.g. NYC Local Law 144 requires an independent bias audit before using an "automated employment decision tool," with mandatory candidate notice. Check a vendor's compliance documentation, not just its accuracy claims, before adopting.
