# Review: Business & Marketing Scaling Concepts

A single review checklist distilling every concept, framework, tactic, and real case study pulled from one long-form business/marketing podcast interview (an agency founder discussing pricing, cash flow, content strategy, client management, and scaling) into notes now living in this vault.

This file was revised once already to go beyond the headline frameworks — a second, sentence-by-sentence pass added the smaller tactical ideas, real numbers, and case studies that the first pass summarized but didn't fully preserve (uncontrolled expansion, owner financial discipline, video creative angles vs. production quality, delivery/return-rate management, client briefing, PR-hiring criteria, agency-switching costs, and more).

## How to use this

- Work through the sections top to bottom — each roughly follows the order the ideas came up in the conversation, grouped by theme rather than timestamp.
- Check a box once you've read the note *and* can restate the idea and its real-world example without looking.
- Every item links to a full concept note — this file is the map, not the content.
- 🆕 = a note created specifically from this review · 🔗 = an existing note the conversation reinforced with a new example, case study, or real numbers · 📘 = a step-by-step guide, not a concept note.
