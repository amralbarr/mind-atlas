# Finding Hook Rate & Hold Rate in Meta Ads Manager

A short how-to for locating the two video-engagement metrics explained in [Paid Social Ad Metrics](../concepts/analytics/paid-social-ad-metrics.md) — most advertisers never find these because they aren't shown by default.

## Steps

1. Open **Ads Manager** and drill down to the **Ads** level (not Campaign or Ad Set level) — these metrics are reported per individual ad creative.
2. Click into a specific ad to open its detail view.
3. Scroll down below the main performance summary.
4. Look toward the **bottom-right** of the ad detail panel — **Hook Rate** and **Hold Rate** are listed there as columns, not headline metrics.
5. If they aren't visible, use the **Columns → Customize Columns** menu and search for "Hook Rate" / "Hold Rate" (naming may appear under a video-engagement metrics section) to add them to the table view.

## What You're Looking At

- **Hook Rate** — the percentage of viewers who watched past the first ~3 seconds.
- **Hold Rate** — the percentage who kept watching into the body of the video (roughly the 10-15 second mark).

See [Paid Social Ad Metrics](../concepts/analytics/paid-social-ad-metrics.md) for how to interpret these once found, and [Video Ad Creative Angles](../concepts/digital-marketing/video-ad-creative-angles.md) for what to change when hook rate specifically is low.
