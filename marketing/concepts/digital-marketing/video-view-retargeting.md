# Video-View-Percentage Retargeting

A specific, high-leverage paid-social technique: building a retargeting audience from people who watched a *specific percentage* of a video, rather than everyone who merely saw an impression — then serving that audience follow-up content matched to where they are in the funnel.

## Why View-Percentage Beats Generic Retargeting

Someone who watched 25%+ of a 3-minute educational video has demonstrated real interest in the topic, not just passive exposure — retargeting that specific group is targeting *intent*, not just reach. Retargeting everyone who was merely shown the ad (regardless of whether they watched a second of it) wastes budget on people who never engaged with the message in the first place.

## A Real Funnel Built This Way

A documented case, run on a near-zero budget over about a month, for a completely new, unproven service (a cosmetic surgery procedure with no prior paid-social track record):

1. **Awareness video** (~3 minutes long, broad targeting) — addressed the underlying problem the service solves, deliberately made long enough that only genuinely interested viewers would watch a meaningful portion. Ran for about a week and organically reached a large audience on minimal spend.
2. **First retargeting audience** — built from people who watched at least 25% of that video, served a second video going deeper (this time covering the solution itself).
3. **Second retargeting layer** — the same audience, refined further, served yet another piece of content continuing the same narrative.
4. **Conversion** — the first paying customer arrived on day 29 of this sequence — illustrating that a properly sequenced funnel can take weeks to convert its first result, which is exactly why a [roadmap and realistic timeframe](../../../management/concepts/client-relationships/client-expectation-management.md) matters when judging whether a campaign is "working."

## The General Pattern

> **Broad awareness content (long enough to filter for real interest) → retarget by view-percentage → serve deeper, next-stage content → repeat → convert.**

This is a concrete implementation of the [customer journey](../fundamentals/customer-journey.md) and [AIDA funnel](../growth/marketing-funnel-aida.md) using paid social's own engagement data (video view %) as the mechanism for moving someone from one stage to the next, rather than relying on separate landing pages or forms to segment intent.

## Common Questions

**How long should the initial awareness video be?**
> Long enough that watching a meaningful portion of it is itself a real signal of interest — a very short video makes "watched 25%" a much weaker filter, since almost anyone scrolling past would clear that bar.

**Does this technique still work on a very small budget?**
> Yes — the documented example ran with minimal spend and still produced a first sale within a month, because the targeting became progressively more precise (and therefore more efficient) at each retargeting stage, rather than spending broadly throughout.
