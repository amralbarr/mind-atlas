# Social Media Marketing

Using social platforms to build audience, distribute content, run paid campaigns, and manage brand reputation. It spans two distinct disciplines that are often confused: **organic** (unpaid posting and community management) and **paid social** (advertising on the platform).

## Organic vs. Paid Social

| | Organic | Paid |
|---|---|---|
| Reach | Limited by algorithm, existing followers | Scales directly with budget |
| Targeting | Whoever follows/sees it | Precise (interests, behavior, lookalikes, retargeting) |
| Speed | Slow to build | Immediate |
| Cost | Time/content investment | Direct ad spend |

Most brands need both: organic builds trust and community over time; paid drives predictable, scalable reach and testing.

## Platform Fit

Choosing platforms should follow the audience, not trends — a B2B SaaS product and a consumer fashion brand rarely belong on the same primary platform. The recurring questions: where does the [target audience](../fundamentals/buyer-personas.md) actually spend time, and does the content format the brand can realistically produce (video, long-form text, visual) fit that platform's native format?

## Community Management

Responding to comments, messages, and mentions — increasingly a customer service and brand reputation function, not just a marketing one. A slow or dismissive public response to a complaint is itself a piece of content every other viewer sees.

## Influencer Marketing

Partnering with individuals who have an engaged audience to promote a product. Roughly spans a spectrum:

| Tier | Followers (rough) | Trade-off |
|---|---|---|
| **Nano** | <10K | High trust/engagement, low reach, low cost |
| **Micro** | 10K-100K | Good balance of engagement and reach |
| **Macro** | 100K-1M | Broad reach, lower relative engagement |
| **Celebrity** | 1M+ | Maximum reach, weakest per-follower trust, highest cost |

Engagement rate (interactions ÷ followers) is usually a better fit signal than raw follower count, since it reflects whether the audience actually pays attention.

## Common Questions

**Why does organic reach keep declining on most platforms?**
> Platforms optimize their feed algorithm for time-on-platform, and increasingly favor content over declared "follow" relationships (recommendation-style feeds) — this pushes brands toward paid to guarantee reach, which is a deliberate incentive built into the platform's business model.

**How should influencer marketing be measured?**
> Beyond vanity metrics (views, likes), tie it to trackable actions — unique discount codes, dedicated landing pages, or affiliate links — so it can be compared against other channels on cost-per-acquisition, not just reach.
