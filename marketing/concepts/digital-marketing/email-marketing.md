# Email Marketing

Marketing delivered directly to a subscriber's inbox. It remains one of the highest-ROI channels because the audience opted in, the channel is owned (unlike a social platform's algorithm), and messaging can be precisely segmented and timed.

## Types of Email Campaigns

| Type | Purpose | Trigger |
|---|---|---|
| **Newsletter** | Ongoing relationship, content, updates | Scheduled (weekly/monthly) |
| **Promotional** | Drive a specific offer or launch | Scheduled, tied to a campaign |
| **Transactional** | Confirms an action the user took | Automatic (order confirmation, password reset) |
| **Drip / nurture sequence** | Move a lead toward a decision over time | Triggered by signup or behavior, sent on a delay |
| **Re-engagement / win-back** | Recover inactive subscribers | Triggered by inactivity |

## Core Metrics

| Metric | Formula | What it signals |
|---|---|---|
| **Open rate** | Opens ÷ delivered | Subject line and sender reputation |
| **Click-through rate (CTR)** | Clicks ÷ delivered | Content and offer relevance |
| **Conversion rate** | Conversions ÷ delivered (or ÷ clicks) | Whether the email actually drove the intended action |
| **Unsubscribe rate** | Unsubscribes ÷ delivered | Content fatigue or targeting mismatch |
| **List growth rate** | Net new subscribers ÷ total list | Health of acquisition relative to churn |

## List Building & Deliverability

- **Permission matters** — purchased lists have poor deliverability and often violate anti-spam law (e.g. CAN-SPAM, GDPR); opted-in lists convert far better.
- **Segmentation** — sending the same email to an entire list underperforms sending relevant emails to smaller, well-defined segments (see [Market Segmentation](../fundamentals/market-segmentation.md)).
- **Deliverability** — depends on sender reputation, [authentication](../../../engineering/concepts/security/auth/authentication.md) (SPF/DKIM/DMARC), and engagement rates; low engagement over time can land future emails in spam even for engaged subscribers.

## Automation & Drip Sequences

A drip sequence is a pre-built series of emails sent on a schedule or trigger (e.g. signup → day 0 welcome → day 3 educational → day 7 offer). This is where email marketing overlaps with the broader [customer journey](../fundamentals/customer-journey.md): each email should match the recipient's current stage rather than pushing a purchase on day one.

## Common Questions

**Why is email marketing considered "owned" media?**
> Unlike organic social reach, which is subject to a platform's algorithm changes, an email list is a direct relationship the business controls — no platform can throttle it.

**What's a healthy open rate?**
> It varies heavily by industry and list quality, so benchmarks should come from the business's own historical data, not generic figures — a sudden drop from a business's own baseline is a more reliable signal than comparing to an industry average.
